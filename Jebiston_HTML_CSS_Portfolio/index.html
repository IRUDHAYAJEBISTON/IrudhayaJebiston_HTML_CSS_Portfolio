🐟 Fisheries Ecosystem
Fisheries Technology | Digital Fisheries Ecosystem Project Concept

BlueNet — Fisheries Management System

BlueNet is a digital fisheries ecosystem concept designed to connect fishermen, customers, and fisheries-related services. It aims to improve access to fish markets, simplify the buying and selling process, and provide useful information and services for fishing communities.

Key Project Objectives

  • Help fishermen reach potential customers directly.
  • Improve access to fair fish price information.
  • Simplify digital fish ordering.
  • Connect users with fisheries-related services.
  • Explore technology solutions for fishing communities.

Interface & System Concepts

  • Digital Fish Marketplace: Display available fish inventory and connect local fishermen with customers.
  • Price Information: Real-time price tracking interface for regional daily market trends.
  • Digital Orders: Simple interface for order management and fulfillment tracking.
  • Fisheries Services: Equipment, boat motors, fishing gear, and maintenance directory.
  • User Dashboards: Dedicated views designed for fishermen, customers, and administrators.

Proposed Future Extensions

  • Weather Information: Maritime alerts and weather conditions interface for coastal activities.
  • Boat Tracking: Vessel location interface concept for safety monitoring.

🖊 Smart Governance
Civic Technology | Smart Governance In Development

CivicEye AI — Public Problem Reporting System

CivicEye AI is a civic technology project designed to help citizens report public problems and help administrators organise, review, and track complaints. The project focuses on making public issue reporting more accessible and improving the visibility of reported problems.

Public Problems Addressed

⚠ Damaged Roads ♻ Waste Accumulation 💡 Broken Streetlights 🏗 Public Infrastructure 📣 Community Concerns

Core System Modules

  • Public Complaint Reporting: Streamlined submission form allowing citizens to describe civic issues.
  • Issue Categorisation: Systematic organisation across roads, waste, lighting, and infrastructure.
  • Complaint Tracking: Transparent status lifecycle showing active review and resolution phases.
  • Administrator Dashboard: Central management interface for sorting, prioritizing, and inspecting reports.

Proposed AI & Analytics Modules

  • Location-Based Issues: Mapping and geolocation tagging for field operations.
  • Duplicate Complaint Detection: AI-assisted text matching to cluster similar community reports.
  • Reports and Visual Summaries: Departmental analytics and complaint resolution metrics.
